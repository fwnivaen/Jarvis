# Cash Flow Forecast

The fictional opening cash balance is $28,000. Monthly fixed cash outflows are projected at $8,400 before food purchases and hourly wages. Rent is $3,200, utilities $550, insurance $250, software $180, founder draws $3,000, and miscellaneous fixed costs $1,220. The minimum cash reserve is $16,800. Maya updates the 13-week forecast every Monday using actual bank activity. Catering deposits improve timing but must not be treated as earned revenue before fulfillment.
