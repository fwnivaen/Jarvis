# Weekly Scorecard

The Monday scorecard tracks net sales, number of orders, average ticket, food cost percentage, labor hours, waste by weight, and returning-customer rate. Week-one sample results were $8,120 net sales from 580 orders, a $14 average ticket, 32% food cost, 148 labor hours, 21 kilograms of food waste, and 17% returning customers. These are fictional sample results. The team should investigate the food-cost gap before increasing the marketing budget.
