# Menu Pricing

The harvest bowl sells for $12.50, the smoky chickpea wrap for $10.50, and the ginger tofu bowl for $13.50. Add avocado for $2 and a house lemonade for $3. Ingredient cost targets are $3.50 for the harvest bowl, $2.80 for the wrap, and $3.90 for the tofu bowl. These costs exclude labor, rent, and packaging. Avoid a blanket menu discount; use a lunch bundle that adds lemonade for $2. Prices should be reviewed monthly against supplier invoices.
