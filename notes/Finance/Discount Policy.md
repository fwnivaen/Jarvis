# Discount Policy

Front-counter staff may replace an incorrect item or issue a refund for the affected item without manager approval. Discounts above 10% require the shift lead. The student bundle offers a bowl plus lemonade with a $1 saving and cannot be stacked with loyalty rewards. Track refunds separately from promotional discounts so operational problems remain visible. Never promise a free catering order; refer those complaints to Maya.
