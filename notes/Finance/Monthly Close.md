# Monthly Close

Maya completes the close by the fifth business day of each month. Reconcile the bank, payment processor, supplier balances, and cash drawer; separate sales tax collected from revenue. Count ending inventory before calculating food usage. Record catering deposits as a liability until orders are delivered. Review the resulting profit and loss statement against the budget and explain large variances in plain language. Retain invoices in a month-labeled folder.
