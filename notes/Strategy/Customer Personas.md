# Customer Personas

The main customer is a student or office worker looking for a quick lunch under $15. Students value filling portions, clear prices, and pickup reliability. Office customers value a predictable 15-minute pickup window and receipts suitable for reimbursement. A smaller weekend segment includes families seeking vegetable-forward meals without strict dietary labels. Interviews with 18 potential customers suggested that taste and speed matter more than the word vegan. Messaging should lead with flavor and convenience.
