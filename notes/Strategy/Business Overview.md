# Business Overview

Juniper Kitchen is a fictional neighborhood plant-based café near a university. Its mission is to make fast, filling lunches accessible without making sustainability feel like a luxury. The café opens Tuesday through Sunday, 11 am to 8 pm. Maya owns the business and handles finance; Leo leads the kitchen. The initial menu has three bowls, two wraps, and a rotating soup. The first-quarter objective is 110 orders a day with an average ticket of $14.50. Growth should come from repeat local customers before a second location is considered.
