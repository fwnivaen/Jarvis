# Quarterly Goals

For the fictional October–December planning quarter, Juniper Kitchen aims to reach 110 orders a day, keep food cost at or below 30% of net food sales, and achieve a 25% returning-customer rate. Maya reviews progress each Monday. Leo owns waste reduction, and Priya owns customer retention. These are targets, not recorded results. A second location is explicitly out of scope until the café produces three consecutive profitable months. The office catering pilot is capped at two bookings per week.
