# Expansion Criteria

Do not sign a second lease yet. Expansion requires three consecutive profitable months, eight weeks of operating cash in reserve, and a shift lead who can run service without either founder present. The current bottleneck is lunchtime packing capacity, not customer awareness. First test an extra packing station and two catering slots per week. Maya should compare the contribution from those changes against their labor cost before proposing any capital commitment.
