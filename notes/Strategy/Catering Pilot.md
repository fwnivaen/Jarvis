# Catering Pilot

The catering pilot serves office teams of 10 to 25 people with a minimum order value of $180. Customers choose two bowl bases and two proteins; sauces and allergens are labeled separately. Orders require 48 hours of notice and a 50% deposit. Delivery is limited to a two-mile radius, with a $20 fee. Priya confirms headcount the day before; Leo checks packing before dispatch. Limit the pilot to two bookings weekly until on-time delivery exceeds 95% for four weeks.
