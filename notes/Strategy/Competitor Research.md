# Competitor Research

The local comparison set includes a salad chain, a deli, and a campus dining hall. The salad chain charges roughly $16 for a bowl but has strong online ordering. The deli offers cheaper sandwiches with few plant-based choices. Campus dining wins on convenience but has limited evening hours. These are fictional planning observations rather than a current market survey. Juniper will compete through warm bowls, honest portions, and clear pickup times rather than constant discounting.
