# Supplier Agreements

Green Acres Farm delivers seasonal produce Tuesday and Friday mornings. Orders close at noon the previous day and payment terms are net 14. City Pantry supplies grains, chickpeas, tofu, and dry goods weekly on Wednesday with net 30 terms. Keep a backup produce vendor for weather disruptions. Leo compares delivered quantities and quality against the purchase order before signing. The café has not committed to exclusive purchasing or minimum annual volume.
