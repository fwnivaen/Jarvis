# Inventory Counts

Count high-value perishables each evening and complete a full inventory Sunday after closing. Record opening quantity, deliveries, usage, spoilage, and closing quantity in the count sheet. Tofu has a two-day safety-stock target; dry grains have a seven-day target. Label containers with received and prepared dates and use the oldest safe stock first. Only Leo approves emergency supplier orders. Investigate repeated variances rather than adjusting counts to match the forecast.
