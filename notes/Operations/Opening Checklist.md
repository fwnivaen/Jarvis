# Opening Checklist

The opening lead checks refrigeration logs, receives deliveries, verifies ingredient labels, and confirms that the handwashing station is stocked. The kitchen prepares the first rice batch and portions sauces before 10:30 am. Front-counter staff test the payment terminal, confirm pickup slots, and restock takeaway packaging. At 10:50 the team holds a five-minute briefing about shortages, allergens, and expected catering orders. Doors open at 11 am.
