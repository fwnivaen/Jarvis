# Allergen Procedure

Maintain an ingredient matrix for every menu item and sauce. Sesame appears in tahini dressing; soy appears in tofu and some sauces. Shared equipment means the café cannot guarantee an allergen-free kitchen. Staff must ask the shift lead before answering an ingredient question and must never guess. For a reported allergy, explain cross-contact limits before accepting the order. Use freshly cleaned utensils and separately labeled packaging following the kitchen’s approved procedure.
