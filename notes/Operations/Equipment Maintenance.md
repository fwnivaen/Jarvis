# Equipment Maintenance

The dishwasher is checked daily for its documented operating conditions, and refrigerator seals are inspected weekly. A qualified service provider performs quarterly refrigeration maintenance. Keep a service log with dates, reported faults, and repairs. If a refrigerator cannot maintain the required safe temperature, isolate affected stock and follow the approved food-safety procedure; do not continue service from that unit. Maya approves equipment purchases above $500.
