# Training Plan

New hires complete three supervised shifts before working independently. Shift one covers handwashing, ingredient labels, allergens, and emergency contacts. Shift two covers recipes, portioning, and order assembly. Shift three covers the payment terminal, pickup handoff, and service recovery. The shift lead signs a practical checklist rather than relying on attendance alone. Staff should practice explaining cross-contact limits without promising an allergen-free kitchen.
