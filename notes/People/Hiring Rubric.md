# Hiring Rubric

Evaluate candidates on reliability, customer communication, food-handling awareness, and ability to follow a recipe. Use the same questions and practical exercise for every candidate. Prior restaurant experience is helpful but not required for the counter role. Do not ask about protected personal characteristics. A paid practical trial should include portioning a sample order and explaining a pickup delay politely. Maya makes the final offer after references are checked with permission.
