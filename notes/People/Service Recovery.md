# Service Recovery

Listen without interrupting, confirm what went wrong, and offer a specific remedy. An incorrect meal can be replaced or refunded for that item by the front-counter team. Escalate serious safety complaints, large catering disputes, or requests outside the discount policy to Maya. Record the cause and action without unnecessary personal details. A customer should hear when the replacement will be ready, not just an apology.
