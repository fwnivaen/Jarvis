# Owner Responsibilities

Maya approves spending above $500, maintains the cash forecast, and owns supplier contracts. Leo owns recipe consistency, ordering, kitchen safety, and waste reviews. Priya owns marketing, customer feedback, and front-counter training. Any founder absence needs a named shift lead and a written handover. Discuss disagreements away from the service counter and document the decision after the weekly meeting. No one may promise a second location or large catering expansion without Maya’s approval.
