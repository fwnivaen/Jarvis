# Team Meeting

The team meets Mondays at 9:30 am for 25 minutes. Start with one customer success, review the weekly scorecard, choose one operating problem, and agree on an owner and deadline. Last week’s sample decision was to add the lunch packer and reduce the opening rice batch. Leo owns the rice change and Priya owns the pickup handoff. Review outcomes the following Monday before adding new experiments.
