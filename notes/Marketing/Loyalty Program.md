# Loyalty Program

Customers earn one stamp per paid main meal. Eight stamps unlock a free standard main, excluding paid extras and catering. Enrollment is optional; collect only the contact details necessary to deliver the reward. Rewards cannot be stacked with the student bundle. Priya reviews participation monthly and checks whether members return more often than nonmembers. A lost stamp card can be replaced only when the purchase history supports it.
