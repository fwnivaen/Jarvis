# Brand Voice

Juniper sounds warm, direct, and a little playful. Lead with roasted vegetables, bright sauces, and satisfying portions. Avoid preaching, guilt-based sustainability claims, or promising medical benefits. The tagline is “Good lunch. Better afternoon.” Use short captions with one clear action, such as reserving a pickup slot. Photography should show the actual portions and packaging customers receive. Explain unfamiliar ingredients without assuming the customer follows a plant-based diet.
