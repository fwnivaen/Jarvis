# Social Calendar

Post three times weekly: Tuesday’s seasonal bowl, Thursday’s kitchen process, and Saturday’s customer or neighborhood story with consent. Priya drafts captions on Monday and Leo checks ingredient accuracy. Each week should include one pickup link and one useful menu explanation. Use actual café photographs rather than stock food imagery. A campaign dashboard records clicks, completed orders, and spend every Friday. Keep the same warm tone across captions and customer replies.
