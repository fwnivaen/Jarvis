# Customer Feedback

The sample feedback set includes 42 receipt-survey responses. Customers praised the ginger tofu bowl and portion size, but six mentioned long pickup waits between noon and 1 pm. Four asked for clearer allergen labels. The response plan is to add a packing role during the lunch rush and put a menu ingredient summary beside pickup. Priya follows up only with customers who requested contact. The survey is directional and too small to represent every customer.
