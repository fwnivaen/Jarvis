# Launch Campaign

The launch campaign uses a $1,200 test budget over three weeks: $500 for local social ads, $300 for sample portions, $250 for photography, and $150 for printed pickup cards. Offer samples outside approved community events rather than unsolicited office visits. Every printed card uses a trackable pickup link. Judge the test on first purchases and repeat visits, not impressions alone. Stop any ad set after $150 of spend if it produces no attributable purchase.
