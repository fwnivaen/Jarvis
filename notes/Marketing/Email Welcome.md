# Email Welcome

The welcome email thanks customers for joining and introduces the three signature mains, opening hours, and pickup link. A second email after seven days explains how the loyalty stamps work. Do not send daily promotions. Each email must include an easy unsubscribe option. Priya writes the copy in the brand voice and measures clicks and completed pickup orders. The welcome offer is the existing lunch bundle rather than a new discount.
